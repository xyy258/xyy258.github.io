This Privacy Policy explains how I handle data on this website.

## Data Collection

This website does not collect any personal data. I do not use:

- Contact forms
- Newsletter signups
- Analytics or tracking tools
- Cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Login or user accounts

Since I don't collect any personal information, I have no data to store, process, or share.

## Visitor Information

Your browser may send certain technical information when you visit (like your IP address and device type), but I do not record, store, or use this information. Your visit leaves no trace on my servers beyond standard web server logs, which I do not monitor or analyze.

## Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process some technical data according to their privacy practices. For details, see [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have rights over your personal data — but since I don't collect any, these rights don't apply here. If you have concerns about your privacy, contact GitHub directly using their privacy procedures.

## Changes to This Policy

If I add analytics, forms, or any data collection in the future, I will update this policy and notify you. You can check this page periodically for updates.
